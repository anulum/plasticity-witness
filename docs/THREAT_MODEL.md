# Data and authority boundaries

## Present tooling

Maintained source, selected executable files and the locked tooling environment are inputs to repository controls. Received descriptors and generated inventories cannot choose policy or execute a learning experiment. Checks inspect bounded local scopes and report fixed diagnostics. Byte identity is separate from numerical or statistical validity and hosted execution.

An inventory is not a concurrent filesystem snapshot or power-failure durability guarantee. Native children use maintained finite argument vectors and direct-child deadlines; completed-output limits are not live volume ceilings. Foundation qualification is in progress.

## Future protocol and observation inputs

Plans, source records, raw traces, numerical profiles and reports are supplied data. They do not grant capture, program execution, signing, equipment, provider, spend or publication authority. Independent reference, numerical applicability and actual producer support precede interpreting them.

Preserve full event/reset/reward order, original and converted units, intermediate states, encoded arrays, RNG state and every attempted population cell. No silent cropping, missing-to-zero conversion, scalar-only equality, merged software/fabric scope or invented fixed-point alias is admissible. Invalid bits must refuse before source state changes.

Unknown reference, state, capture, source/error method or current trust cannot become positive acceptance. A signature does not prove independent reference or learning correctness. Shared evidence, equipment and operational recovery remain separate unimplemented domain boundaries; no isolation, physical, scientific or durability guarantee is claimed here.
